🎨 Reimagine

Paste any HTML page and Reimagine rebuilds it in 17 directions — infographic, 3D, cinematic, dashboard, lookbook, particles and more — from the nouns, dates, numbers, emails and colors already in your source. Nothing invented, nothing uploaded: it runs entirely in your browser tab, and as a CLI (npx reimagine-it).

▶ Open the live playground ★ GitHub — reimagine-it

17 directions, one source

Why it's different

  • Content-derived. Palette, motif and motion come from the source itself — a bakery can't come out marine-teal.
  • Nothing invented. Facts, dates, numbers and emails in the source stay in the redesign; a fidelity floor test enforces it.
  • Offline & private. One standalone HTML file out. No accounts, no uploads, no telemetry.
  • For agents too. CLI (--auto, variations, audit) plus a skill for Claude, Cursor, Codex and Copilot.

Try it in seconds

The playground lives at navigatorslab.com/reimagine/ — paste HTML, hit Auto, pick a direction, download the redesign. Or run it locally:

npx reimagine-it --auto -i page.html -o redesign.html
