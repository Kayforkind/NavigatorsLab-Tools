🔳 QR Studio

Make QR codes from text or links, and read QR codes out of images — both fully in your browser. The decoder runs on-device (jsQR), so a Wi-Fi password, a tracking link, or anything else you scan never leaves your machine.

Drop a QR image or click to pick — PNG / JPG / WebP, decoded on-device
